## Key Findings
1. Average CAC is **232.42**, which is **~54.9% higher** than the industry target (150).
2. The quarterly CACs show a rising trend (slope > 0), indicating costs are increasing quarter-over-quarter.
3. If left unaddressed, rising CAC will pressure margins and require reallocation of marketing spend or changes in customer value economics.

## Business Implications
- Sustained CAC above benchmark implies marketing efficiency issues and potential over-investment in high-cost channels.
- Executive attention is needed to avoid long-term erosion of marketing ROI and margin compression.
- Quick wins can recover significant cost reductions; longer-term investments (SEO, content, retention) will reduce CAC sustainably.

## Recommendation — Optimize digital marketing channels
We recommend the following immediate and medium-term actions to reach the target CAC = 150:

### Immediate (0–3 months)
- Reallocate spend away from the worst-performing paid channels.
- Pause low-conversion campaigns; reassign budget to channels with historically lower CAC.
- Run rapid A/B tests on creative and landing pages to boost conversion rate.

### Short/Medium (3–9 months)
- Implement granular audience segmentation and bid strategies.
- Strengthen conversion tracking and attribution to identify true channel ROI.
- Invest in SEO and content to build organic acquisition, reducing paid CAC over time.

### Long-term (9–18 months)
- Focus on retention and increasing LTV (improves sustainable CAC).
- Develop owned channels (email, community) to reduce dependency on paid acquisition.
